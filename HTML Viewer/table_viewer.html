Historical Results

Exact expiry is counted in observed H1 market bars, not calendar days.

Each signal view has 4 stop widths × 13 independent target widths = 52 cells per horizon. A 4:4 cell means SL=4 ATR and TP=4 ATR, not TP=16 ATR. These are parameter labels only. N, TP-first, SL-first, timeout, gross R and ambiguity remain blank until a versioned ledger is independently audited. Historical gross OHLC proxies are not broker fills or proof of future profitability.
